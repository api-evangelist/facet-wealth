## About
Facet is a national SEC-registered investment advisor (RIA) and financial planning firm that provides personalized, fiduciary financial advice through a membership-based model. 
Founded in 2016, Facet helps individuals and families manage their full financial lives through comprehensive financial planning, investment management, retirement planning, tax strategy, tax preparation and filing, equity compensation planning, insurance guidance, and estate planning. Facet combines a proprietary technology platform with a team of CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER professionals to deliver advice that is objective, personalized and not tied to product sales.

## Mission
Facet's mission is to help people make confident financial decisions and live more enriched lives through accessible, comprehensive financial planning.

Facet provides fiduciary financial advice through a flat annual membership model rather than commissions or asset-based fees. Members receive an ongoing financial roadmap that evolves as goals, income and life circumstances change.
